BlinkenBadge Privacy

Updated October 28, 2026.

Local Data

BlinkenBadge stores your working project and display preferences on your device. It does not require an account or send projects, imported images, or badge identifiers to developer-operated servers.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth access finds nearby compatible badges and sends artwork to the badge you select. On Android 7 through 11, the operating system also requires location permission for Bluetooth discovery. BlinkenBadge does not use this permission to track your location.

Files

BlinkenBadge reads the projects and images you select with the system picker. Export writes a copy to the destination you choose. Cloud storage providers handle exported files under their own privacy policies.

Tracking

BlinkenBadge includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking services. Apple and Google may provide platform diagnostics and beta-testing feedback under their own policies and your device settings.

Retention

Your working project remains on the device until you replace it or remove the app's local data. Exported copies remain in the destination you selected until you delete them there.
